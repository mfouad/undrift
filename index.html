Chrome only lets a page make sound after you have clicked or typed on it once since it loaded. Until then the chip at the top says “Click to enable sound”. The chime runs only while this page is open in a tab.

Engines

Put %s where the question goes. An engine without %s (like the Gemini app) gets the question copied to the clipboard instead — paste it with Ctrl+V. The dot marks the default.

Groups

On the page: + adds a bookmark, right-click a bookmark to edit or delete it, drag to reorder or move it to another group. The ⧉ button opens a whole group; Chrome will ask you to allow pop-ups for this site the first time.

Everything is stored in this browser's localStorage. Export a copy to keep it safe or move it to another computer.
